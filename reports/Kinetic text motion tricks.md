# Master Kinetic Text With New Tricks

The decisive 2025-2026 shift is that **variable-font axes animated directly in After Effects 26 and the browser, GSAP's SplitText and MorphSVG going entirely free on 29 April 2025, and Cavalry's audio and physics Behaviours made per-character physics, weight morphs and mask reveals the baseline**, not the showpiece. Video and web stacks have converged on the same tricks — stagger, weight morph, mask reveal, audio drive — and the tool now gets chosen by output, with After Effects and Cinema 4D still the cinematic standard while **Cavalry, Rive, GSAP/WebGL and Blender own iteration, interaction and simulation** ([Source](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13)) ([Source](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/01/22/whats-new-in-adobe-after-effects-26-0/)) ([Source](https://www.svgator.com/blog/kinetic-typography-a-guide-to-text-in-motion/)). The result is a new toolkit where **slit-scan time-displacement warps, falloff-limited deformers, shader dissolves and scroll-scrubbed SplitText do the expressive work that hand-keyframed scale and skew used to fake**, and AI reliably builds camera, lighting and B-roll plates while legible words still get composited in post because text-to-video warps letters and breaks speed continuity ([Source](https://www.toolfarm.com/tutorial/in-depth-time-effects-time-displacement-aka-slit-scan-with-examples/)) ([Source](https://crepal.ai/blog/aivideo/pika-2-5-tutorial-master-dynamic-camera-prompts-2025)). A designer who learns one trick family from each stack — variable axes, spring physics, warp masks, scroll choreography and shader text — covers nearly every advanced tutorial published this year.

## Variable fonts now animate weight, width, and slant on their own

**After Effects 26 shipped native keyframing of weight, width, slant and wiggle axes**, ending the beta era in which Type Morph scripts were the workaround for creating and animating variable fonts ([Source](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/01/22/whats-new-in-adobe-after-effects-26-0/)) ([Source](https://maketypework.com/after-effects-animate-variable-fonts/)) ([Source](https://aescripts.com/learn/post/how-to-make-variable-fonts-in-after-effects?srsltid=AYCX9vJV3Qg3360dvSEok1tnEu1AFdU2Uxlf04XGPLN281ldu6XZ4o94)). Adobe's own variable-font axis documentation adds a **Variable Font Spacing property that controls spacing while axes animate**, which makes tracking and weight now combinable in a single animator instead of two stacked fakes ([Source](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/variable-font-axes/work-with-variable-font-axes/working-with-variable-font-axes.html)). **Browser support for variable fonts crossed 95% globally in 2025**, so the same .ttf variable file now works in After Effects, GSAP on the web via `fontVariationSettings` and Cavalry without re-typesetting ([Source](https://www.pravinkumar.co/blog/variable-fonts-webflow-design-systems-2026)) ([Source](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/21159-animating-variable-fonts/)). The practical change is letterform integrity: **variable axes replace scale and skew faux-bold because the glyphs redraw from the font itself**, so a word can breathe in weight and width while staying crisp on export ([Source](https://maketypework.com/after-effects-animate-variable-fonts/)).

Make Type Work's December 2025 breakdowns make the technique concrete by test driving **7 variable fonts including Adapt Variable, Scatterplot, Superscore and Obviously Variable through a text animator Range Selector offset driven by loopOut pingpong** ([Source](https://maketypework.com/after-effects-animate-variable-fonts/)). **Custom axes now carry effects that used to need deformers**, with Scatterplot's random spread axis scattering letterforms and Superscore's flux axis controlling dot variance while weight and width animate underneath ([Source](https://maketypework.com/after-effects-animate-variable-fonts/)). **Auto Variable Font reads the available axes and generates sine, audio-driven and loop animations in clicks without manual keyframes**, which collapses the setup that Make Type Work does by hand into a panel ([Source](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DRUeiTtjFqH/?hl=en)). For the web, the same move appears as inline style control, so **GSAP animates variable fonts via fontVariationSettings and players drive a companion `--wght` custom property** rather than swapping font files ([Source](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/21159-animating-variable-fonts/)).

That weight morph is the fluid half of the taxonomy tutorials still teach in June 2026: **motion typography moves whole letters intact while fluid typography stretches, morphs and dissolves letterforms in place** ([Source](https://www.svgator.com/blog/kinetic-typography-a-guide-to-text-in-motion/)). **SVG path morph belongs to the fluid side**, where a single letterform abandons its identity to become another, with After Effects, Cinema 4D, Houdini and Unreal still listed as the fluid-transition tools ([Source](https://www.upskillist.com/blog/top-7-kinetic-typography-trends-2025/)). **Tracking animation remains the cheap emotional lever**, because keyframing Tracking amount from negative to zero makes words expand or breathe without re-layout ([Source](https://edricstudio.com/kinetic-typography-animation/)) ([Source](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/animating-kerning-tracking/)). **Kerning itself is still not easily animatable in After Effects or Premiere**, so the new workflow pairs variable width with tracking instead of per-pair kerning passes ([Source](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/animating-kerning-tracking/)) ([Source](https://www.reddit.com/r/videography/comments/64qa9u/animate_text_kerning_in_premiere/)).

## Springs, falloffs, and morph physics have replaced manual easing

**Material 3 Expressive from May 2025 replaced duration and easing with physics-based springs**, using spatial springs that mirror real movement for position and effects springs for color and opacity, tokenized into standard and expressive motion schemes with faster and slower springs plus a built-in square-to-circle shape morph ([Source](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works)) ([Source](https://m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-expressive)). **GSAP 3.14 from 8 December 2025 shipped MorphSVG smooth 80 or auto with redraw false, improved anchor spacing and a new curveMode true that avoids mid-morph kinks**, which means a word can morph through an unrelated glyph without the wobble that made morphs read as errors ([Source](https://gsap.com/blog/3-14)). **Cavalry packages the same idea as Magic Easing alongside Connect Shapes, Rubber Hose, Duplicator, Falloffs, Forge Dynamics and Text Animation**, and its Morph Behaviour is taught specifically as perfect shape morphs between forms ([Source](https://cavalry.studio/en)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s0E2csEnv8Q)). The advanced workflow these three stacks share is no longer better cubic-beziers but a **spring plus token plus morph system where stiffness, damping and smoothing replace hand-drawn graph-editor curves** ([Source](https://gsap.com/blog/3-14)) ([Source](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works)).

**Cavalry Falloffs limit a deformer's influence by shape-type and input-shape and support multiple falloffs on one behaviour**, which lets a wave cross a headline and fade out before the last two letters ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYDu773AHaU)). **Forge Dynamics adds Starts Asleep, Allows Sleep, Starting Velocity and Starting Rotational Velocity plus fields and behaviours for full simulations**, so letters can idle asleep and wake on collision instead of always flying on a path ([Source](https://cavalry.studio/docs/nodes/shapes/forge-dynamics/forge-dynamics-shape)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTILJIlrMVw)). **Blender reaches the same place through Simulation Zones in Geometry Nodes, where frame N influences frame N plus 1 for custom physics**, with the 5.2 LTS manual documenting Simulation Nodes and Baking and the September 2025 workshop still listing declarative force-field and collider systems as active work ([Source](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/physics/simulation_nodes.html)) ([Source](https://code.blender.org/2025/10/geometry-nodes-workshop-september-2025)). **Motionographer's 25 for 2025 predictions put custom physics simulations at trend number 9, bending gravity, wind and friction in Houdini, Blender and Unreal Engine 5 and pointing AI-assisted hyper-personalised simulations as next** ([Source](https://motionographer.com/2025/01/02/25-for-2025-the-motionographers-crystal-ball-motion-design-predictions-part-2)). The same prediction names **kinetic typography at trend number 7, with words as emotive performers rooted in Saul Bass that leap, twist and pulse**, aided by After Effects and Cinema 4D and headed toward 3D tactile text, interactive letters and AI mood-based animation ([Source](https://motionographer.com/2025/01/02/25-for-2025-the-motionographers-crystal-ball-motion-design-predictions-part-2)).

Data-driven control is what makes those systems scale to systems rather than single headlines, because **Cavalry connects a spreadsheet and animates at scale with real-time rendering, summed up in its own promise to change it once and watch it ripple** ([Source](https://cavalry.studio/en)). **Studio Dumbar and DEPT rebuilt motion systems labelled Techniques, Physics, Dynamic Layouts, Animated Glyph and Kinetic Typography in Cavalry from spreadsheets and APIs**, then distributed bespoke systems through Cavalry Control, which is the working example of a brand existing in permanent motion as a recipe and toolkit ([Source](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-motion-design-morphing-the-next-era-graphic-design-150125)). **Loops are treated as a system rather than an afterthought**, because the Cinema 4D rule that **the last frame must lead back to the first with linear keys for constant rotation or scroll versus spline for organic motion and matched velocities** now governs how social kinetic type stays loopable ([Source](https://novedge.com/blogs/design-news/cinema-4d-tip-cinema-4d-seamless-loop-animation-workflow)). After Effects teaching repeats the pattern as **keyframe position and rotation, apply Easy Ease, then loopOut for the cycle and a ping-pong variation for the bounce** ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZtosVOe-XvM)), and **Ben Marriott's flawless-loops system builds an entire advanced morphing transition on that exact rule** ([Source](https://www.benmarriott.com/mastermotiondesign)).

## Slit-scan warps, masks, and seamless loops define the cinematic tricks

**Slit-scan, also documented as time-displacement warping, stretches footage into ribbons and is the 2025 breakout distortion technique** ([Source](https://www.toolfarm.com/tutorial/in-depth-time-effects-time-displacement-aka-slit-scan-with-examples/)). **Styleforces stretches kinetic type with a slit-scan loop, and the tunnel text warp and kinetic warp variants use the same ribbon and tunnel geometry for type** ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c13_lUSzX2I)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlM9hpL3hC4)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvTXbHLtod4)). **Animated mask expansion remains the central reveal underneath the warp**, exemplified by a footprint landing built from multiple ellipse masks animated in sequence from forefoot to toes to heel, then organic-ised with matte plus Roughen Edges and Simple Choker and knocked out by texture with Levels for silhouette and luma control ([Source](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-after-effects-part-1)). **Preserve Underlying Transparency is the 2025 short-form clipping shortcut that replaces a stack of track mattes for layered type reveals** ([Source](https://www.tiktok.com/@justinserran/video/7096191485397323013?lang=en)). Together the warp and the mask give the current cinematic look a distinct division of labour: **the warp transports the eye while the mask controls when the word is allowed to read** ([Source](https://www.toolfarm.com/tutorial/in-depth-time-effects-time-displacement-aka-slit-scan-with-examples/)) ([Source](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-after-effects-part-1)).

Procedural looping is the format that packages those cinematic tricks for social delivery, because **a 99 percent procedural loop built with the CC Griddler 5-minute technique is now a trending advanced After Effects format** ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UmD8FtH8q1A)) ([Source](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSav6KBjiK7/)). **Mt. Mograph Motion Studio carries 80 plus tools for After Effects, Premiere, Illustrator and DaVinci, with a Kinetic tool of velocity-driven animation controllers**, and **Motion version 4.0.5 adds Emitter, Kinetic and Snapshot alongside that suite** ([Source](https://mtmograph.com/products/motion)) ([Source](https://mtmograph.com/products/motion/tools/kinetic)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XiFtUaitqag)). **Mograph Effector 2.0 brings the Cinema 4D Cloner into After Effects**, which closes the gap between 2D kinetic type and 3D text runs ([Source](https://www.facebook.com/schoolofmotion/videos/mograph-effector-20-c4ds-cloner-now-in-after-effects-motion-mondays/2008341053157931/)). **Flow packages advanced easing and motion control as one-click curves without opening the graph editor**, which is the plugin answer to the spring-token shift described above ([Source](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DSxBBZ-gqnB)).

For learning those cinematic tricks the sourced shortlist is tight, because **Motion Nations published a no-plugins Typography Motion Graphics tutorial with 657K views that TryDeMotion cited in May 2026 as a complete After Effects workflow for timing, easing and layered animation**, and its playlist confirms an ongoing series ([Source](https://trydemotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-secrets)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXBMRUjT72cs)). **Panter, shown as Styleforces on the channel, owns the slit-scan stretch and stretch, warp, glitch and distort series** ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c13_lUSzX2I)) ([Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FwgDpFqBny4)). **School of Motion's three-part Kinetic Typography in After Effects series teaches precomping, audio sync and markers, camera moves, mask-expansion reveals, CC Reptile tiling, guide layers and Roughen Edges organic masks**, which makes it the long-form foundation ([Source](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-after-effects-part-1)). **Motion Design School's Animated Typefaces with Animography course teaches flexible typographic systems for clients including Nike, Adidas and NatWest**, which is the specialist typeface-animation path ([Source](https://motiondesign.school/courses/animated-typefaces-with-animography/)). **No reliable source verified dated 2025-2026 kinetic-type-specific tutorials for Adam Tabour or a systematic most-cited ranking beyond the Motion Nations view count**, so the hierarchy above should be read as an informed shortlist from homepage positioning and citations rather than a measured chart ([Source](https://trydemotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-secrets)).

## GSAP going free and CSS scroll timelines shipped theatrical web text

**GSAP 3.13 on 29 April 2025 made GSAP 100 percent free including every Club plugin, SplitText and MorphSVG, through the Webflow acquisition**, with installation now via the public npm package ([Source](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13)). **The SplitText rewrite is 50 percent smaller with 14 new features including aria-label accessibility, autoSplit and onSplit responsive re-splitting, deepSlice through nested elements, masking, propIndex, emoji and foreign-character accuracy, smartWrap and a standalone mode** ([Source](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13)). The working number that recurs in the 2026 web guides is timing: **GSAP SplitText splits text into chars, words and lines and a stagger of 30 to 50 milliseconds per char feels fluid while 100 milliseconds and up reads as dramatic**, with a limit of **2 to 3 split elements per page because 50 chars become 50 spans and mobile jank follows** ([Source](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/SplitText/)) ([Source](https://studiomeyer.io/en/blog/kinetische-typografie)). **SplitText stalls when custom fonts load late, so the 2025 community thread tells designers to wait for document fonts or use autoSplit**, which fixes the jank that otherwise makes meticulously timed staggers misalign ([Source](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/44907-improving-reliability-and-performance-of-splittext-elements-and-scrubbed-timeline-animation)).

**CSS scroll-driven animation shipped in Chrome 115 in August 2023 and now runs across all major browsers, offloading to the compositor at roughly zero JavaScript cost and surviving mobile better than requestAnimationFrame JavaScript** ([Source](https://www.3str.net/blog/kinetic-typography-in-web-design)). **NRK's February 2025 case study animated a scroll-driven title with animation fade-up linear, animation-timeline view and animation-range entry 100 percent to exit 100 percent for scrollytelling** ([Source](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/nrk-casestudy)). **The production pattern is CSS animation-timeline scroll root as progressive enhancement with animation-range entry 0 percent to entry 40 percent, and GSAP ScrollTrigger only for complex scrub and pin timelines**, because video and GIF are now a last resort for fixed resolution ([Source](https://www.3str.net/blog/kinetic-typography-in-web-design)). That split between free baseline and choreographed enhancement explains why **the 2026 Studio Meyer guide restricts web animation to transform and opacity for GPU compositing, bans width, height, top, left, margin and font-size because they force reflow, requires prefers-reduced-motion respect, and subsets variable fonts with glyphanger or pyftsubset** ([Source](https://studiomeyer.io/en/blog/kinetische-typografie)).

The showpiece web tricks all build on that baseline, beginning with the **Codrops November 2025 no-Three.js cylinder built with perspective 70vw, preserve-3d and translate3d plus rotateX, pinned and scrubbed from rotateX minus 80 to 270 over plus equals 2000svh**, with **a mirrored double circle that adds rotate plus 180 degrees to fix right-side readability and a tube that rotates rotateY zero to 360 for a tunnel** ([Source](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/11/04/creating-3d-scroll-driven-text-animations-with-css-and-gsap/)). **Mouse physics is now a standard dependency**, because Fancy Components Gravity builds a Matter.js world with 4 walls, **MouseConstraint stiffness 0.1 and damping 0.15, and clamps the angle to keep pills upright while the DOM stays crisp** ([Source](https://www.fancycomponents.dev/docs/components/physics/gravity)). **A 2026 cursor micro-interactions showcase lists magnetic buttons, canvas particle trails, text repulsion where chars push from the cursor, image distortion ripple, ripple click, drag physics throw and bounce, elastic scroll and spotlight gallery as copy-paste starters** ([Source](https://github.com/KaranChandekar/cursor-micro-interactions)). **Variable-font mouse control couples Splitting.js with a GSAP tween of a companion wght property in yoyo stagger each 0.2 and quickTo on mousemove**, which turns a headline into a weight instrument without re-splitting on every move ([Source](https://codepen.io/samvgm/pen/PoZGeVB)) ([Source](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/32561-animate-variable-font-on-mouse-move)).

Tool choice on the web is now format-driven, because **After Effects remains the most requested job skill while Cavalry, Rive and Spline gain traction for real-time animation, interactive motion and clean UI workflows** ([Source](https://www.todaymade.com/blog/best-tools-for-motion-design)). **Canva acquired Cavalry on 23 to 25 February 2026, with the 4-person Scene Group team joining Canva and Cavalry keeping independent operation plus integration into Canva core and Affinity, after Canva bought Affinity in 2024 and made it free in October 2025** ([Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/23/canva-acquires-cavalry-for-motion-graphics-and-mangoai-for-video-ads.html)) ([Source](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/02/canva-acquires-next-gen-motion-graphics-tool-cavalry)). **Cavalry is free for individuals with procedural rigs, real-time viewport, data-driven sequences and no restrictions, requiring a Canva account**, which lowers the procedural barrier for kinetic type that used to need a timeline ([Source](https://cavalry.studio/en)). **Rive won the opposite half of the verdict: anything the user touches is Rive rendered in real time, while Cavalry wins rendered output like explainers, generative brand systems, broadcast and Lottie**, because rebuilding a reactive Rive piece as a linear render strands the interaction ([Source](https://artofstyleframe.com/blog/rive-vs-cavalry-which-motion-tool)).

## Shader dissolves, faux-3D, and AI plates change what you bother keyframing

**Cavalry 2.5 Spherise maps an input shape onto a sphere with rotation, inside visibility, lighting, shadow and bump for faux 3D**, and **Cavalry 2.6 beta in December 2025 added text and texture deformers, with the Wave Deformer applied to letters and combined with Forge Dynamics in the demos** ([Source](https://medium.com/cavalry-animation/whats-new-in-cavalry-2-5-70441e6b3703)) ([Source](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-mondays-december-8-2025)) ([Source](https://www.instagram.com/p/DScCB5rDXGd)). **Three.js plus GLSL letter displacement computes distance from a raycast hit point to world position, maps that distance into an easeInOutCubic factor and displaces on Z**, with denser plane geometry for smoothness and a second plane for illumination and shadow blur to fake depth ([Source](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/03/24/animating-letters-with-shaders-interactive-text-effect-with-three-js-glsl)). **WebGPU Gommage dissolves MSDF text with a noise-driven TSL shader plus dust InstancedMesh, spinning petal particles and selective bloom, packing Birth, Life, Seed and Scale into one instanced attribute to respect WebGPU limits** ([Source](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/01/28/webgpu-gommage-effect-dissolving-msdf-text-into-dust-and-petals-with-three-js-tsl/)). **The countertype three-text library renders fonts with the Slug algorithm at one quad per glyph with winding-number coverage that stays sharp at any zoom, then exposes glyphIndex, glyphCenter and time per glyph so a shader can add sin wave motion with a single line** ([Source](https://github.com/countertype/three-text)). **VFX-JS converts a span or h1 through an SVG foreignObject to a canvas to a WebGL texture and applies a shader with one line, using mouse velocity mixed with delay for RGB shift**, which makes it the fastest prototype before committing to an MSDF atlas pipeline ([Source](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/01/20/vfx-js-webgl-effects-made-easy)).

Rive carries the runtime text half of the same shift, because **Rive added text run style animation in January and Text Follow Path in April, aligning text along a shape and binding it to data**, and **runtime text goes through Data Binding so color, size, text, images and lists change at runtime with auto-layout reflow, while a Lottie still needs vector conversion with no runtime text** ([Source](https://community.rive.app/c/announcements/text-follow-path-release)) ([Source](https://rive.app/blog/rive-as-a-lottie-alternative)). **Rive cites Duolingo at 15 times smaller, Spotify Wrapped final animation built in Rive, a Dropbox brand site in Rive and a Wix Studio integration as proof of production use** ([Source](https://rive.app/blog/press)). **Rive's accessibility work shows DOM-live animations adjusting color and speed, layout backgrounds fitting text, and timeline playback at zero times for user control to reach WCAG AAA animation from interactions while holding 60 frames per second and at most 3 flashes per second** ([Source](https://rive.app/blog/making-rive-designs-more-visually-accessible)). The tradeoff is explicit in the notes: **no source retrieved compares Lottie, Rive and SVGator export quality for variable-font preservation in 2026**, so that fidelity table remains an open verification step rather than a claim.

AI is the remaining trick, and the working split is now stable: **Runway Gen-4.5 launched on 1 December 2025 as text-to-video with 1,247 Elo on Artificial Analysis and claims unprecedented physical accuracy with weight and momentum, plus Image-to-Video, Keyframes and Video-to-Video modes** ([Source](https://runway.com/research/introducing-runway-gen-4.5)). **Runway Aleph from July 2025 is in-context instruction editing that changes objects, viewpoint, lighting and style, and Act-Two drives full body, head, face and hand animation without markers for 30-second clips** ([Source](https://www.scriptbyai.com/runway-ai-timeline)) ([Source](https://www.artofsm.art/t/runway-act-2-is-here-full-body-motion-tracking/11169)). **Pika 2.2 offers 720p to 1080p up to 10 seconds, Pikaframes keyframe interpolation from 1 to 10 seconds per transition with up to 5 keyframes and 25 seconds total, Pikascenes for multi-image scene building, Pikadditions and Pikaswaps for inserting or swapping elements in existing video, plus 16 one-click effects from Cake-ify to Explode and Squish** ([Source](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/introducing-pika-v2-2-pikaframes-on-wavespeedai)) ([Source](https://docs.1min.ai/docs/api/ai-for-video/image-to-video/pika-ai-image-to-video)). **The Pika 2.5 field test that worked best used one concrete camera motion per shot and the typography plate tip was to not generate text in Pika at all, but to generate a clean surface shot and overlay text in post** ([Source](https://crepal.ai/blog/aivideo/pika-2-5-tutorial-master-dynamic-camera-prompts-2025)).

That post-overlay rule is enforced by measurement, because **an independent review scored Pika 2.2 at 1.9 out of 10 for prompt adherence, 1.4 for temporal consistency and 1.4 for motion quality, with props that appear and vanish, stutter and jitter, and morphing warps** ([Source](https://curiousrefuge.com/blog/pika-22-ai-video-generator-review)). **HeyGen moves that rule into fast captions by turning any headline or transcript into animated typography that times its own entrances, exits and emphasis for mute-first Reels and LinkedIn** ([Source](https://www.heygen.com/en-au/tool/ai-motion-graphics)). **The After Effects AI Assistant route automates the same sync by prompting a script that scales a text layer to 110 percent at every marker on the Audio layer and eases back to 100 percent, or makes a background shape auto-match the width and height of the text layer**, which puts AI on timing and matching while the designer keeps the axes ([Source](https://www.motionist.studio/post/master-kinetic-typography-in-2025-using-ai-to-automate-fast-paced-text-visuals)). **The Adobe variable-font beta from 20 November 2025 is the bridge, because Text Animators now drive stretch, squash and jitter through variable axes** ([Source](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-532/introducing-variable-font-animation-in-after-effects-beta-26-0x40-314512)). **The business pressure underneath all of it is that 85 percent and more of short-form video gets watched muted, so text must do the talking with pops, slides, shakes and transforms matched to the energy of the spoken word** ([Source](https://www.flowi.video/blog/kinetic-typography-101)).

## Conclusion

The real change is not a new preset but a new division of labour that the 2025-2026 tutorials make unavoidable, and it rewires what a designer should learn first. Weight, width and scroll position are now addressable properties with tokens, springs and spreadsheet values instead of baked keyframes, so **a single variable font plus a falloff plus a scroll timeline produces a family of headlines that used to be a weekend of precomps**. That inverts the old hierarchy: the scarce skill is no longer timeline speed but deciding where the word stops being transport and starts being material, because only the warp, the morph and the shader dissolve can justify that choice while everything else can be driven, looped or retimed.

The next edge is the boundary between driven text and rendered text, and 2026 leaves it deliberately unfinished in the sourced record. **No verification of Lottie versus Rive versus SVGator variable-font fidelity, no measured Cavalry versus After Effects adoption rates, and no independent pricing or broadcast-adoption comparison for AI caption tools were retrieved**, all of which are vendor self-reports in the current notes. Until those fidelity and adoption checks close, the safest architecture is a split one: keep legible copy as live HTML, Rive or variable-font text that can restyle at runtime, and spend render time only on the slit-scan ribbons, MSDF dust and warp plates that live text cannot perform.
