# Advanced Motion Design Workflows and Tutorials 2025-2026

## Advanced easing / physics / morphing workflows being taught in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The advanced workflow in 2025-2026 is no longer “better keyframes” but spring/physics simulation, procedural morphing, and data-driven systems — driven by Cavalry’s Forge Dynamics/Magic Easing/Falloffs, GSAP’s free SplitText/MorphSVG, Material 3 Expressive’s spring tokens, and Blender’s Geometry Nodes Simulation Zones.

### Cited Findings
- GSAP 3.13 (Apr 29, 2025) made GSAP 100% free including all Club plugins — SplitText, MorphSVG — via Webflow acquisition — [GSAP 3.13 release](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13)
- GSAP 3.13 SplitText rewrite: 50% smaller, 14 new features, aria-label accessibility, autoSplit/onSplit responsive re-splitting, deepSlice through nested elements, masking, propIndex, emoji/foreign-character accuracy, smartWrap, standalone mode — [GSAP 3.13 release](https://gsap.com/blog/3-13)
- GSAP 3.14 (Dec 8, 2025) MorphSVG `smooth: 80 | "auto"` with `redraw:false`, improved anchor spacing, new `curveMode:true` to avoid mid-morph kinks, plus new Demo Hub — [GSAP 3.14 Release](https://gsap.com/blog/3-14)
- Material 3 Expressive (May 2025) replaces duration/easing with physics-based springs: spatial springs mirror real movement, effects springs for color/opacity; tokenized standard/expressive motion schemes with faster/slower springs; built-in shape morph (e.g. square→circle) integrated into components — [Material 3 Expressive motion](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works); [Start building with M3 Expressive](https://m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-expressive)
- Cavalry feature stack marketed as professional depth: Magic Easing, Connect Shapes, Rubber Hose, Duplicator, Falloffs, Forge Dynamics, Quad Tree, Text Animation, Data Import, Lottie Export — [Cavalry](https://cavalry.studio/en)
- Cavalry Falloffs workflow taught 2025-2026: oscillator/deformer + falloff (shape-type, input-shape) to limit influence, sub-mesh info, multiple falloffs on one behaviour, looped falloff animation — [Falloffs | Cavalry Animation Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYDu773AHaU)
- Cavalry Forge Dynamics shape: Starts Asleep / Allows Sleep / Starting Velocity / Starting Rotational Velocity, fields & behaviours for physics simulations — [Forge Dynamics Shape docs](https://cavalry.studio/docs/nodes/shapes/forge-dynamics/forge-dynamics-shape); [Mastering Fields in Cavalry's Forge Dynamics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTILJIlrMVw)
- Cavalry Morph Behaviour for animating between shapes taught as “Perfect Shape Morphs in After Effects. A Cavalry” feature — [Using Cavalry to morph between shapes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s0E2csEnv8Q)

- Motionographer 25 (+1) for 2025 Pt.2 predicts custom physics simulations as trend #9: bend gravity/wind/friction via simulation tools, tools cited Houdini/Blender/Unreal Engine 5, AI-assisted hyper-personalised simulations next — [Motionographer 25 for 2025 Pt.2](https://motionographer.com/2025/01/02/25-for-2025-the-motionographers-crystal-ball-motion-design-predictions-part-2)
- Motionographer 25 Pt.2 kinetic typography trend #7: words as emotive performers rooted in Saul Bass, aided by After Effects + Cinema 4D, lyric videos (Billie Eilish), Succession titles, TikTok overlays; future: 3D tactile text, interactive letters, AI mood-based animation — [Motionographer 25 for 2025 Pt.2](https://motionographer.com/2025/01/02/25-for-2025-the-motionographers-crystal-ball-motion-design-predictions-part-2)

### Inferences
- Physics-based springs + tokenized easing (M3 Expressive, GSAP, Cavalry Magic Easing) has replaced manual cubic-bezier hand-tuning for UI/web; for film/brand motion the advanced stack is simulation (Forge Dynamics, Blender Simulation Nodes, Houdini) + morph (MorphSVG smooth/curveMode, Cavalry Morph Behaviour, M3 shape morph).
- Data-driven motion (Cavalry Data Import/spreadsheet, Rive ViewModels/data binding, GSAP propIndex/CSS variables) is the differentiator for scale/personalisation; Motionographer’s AI-personalised content trend #10 aligns with this — [Motionographer 25 for 2025 Pt.2](https://motionographer.com/2025/01/02/25-for-2025-the-motionographers-crystal-ball-motion-design-predictions-part-2)
- Looping is treated as a system (linear vs spline, velocity matching, vessel/loop tools), not an afterthought, especially for social/kinetic-type assets.
